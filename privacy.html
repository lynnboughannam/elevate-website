Who we are

Elevate Estates is a real estate agency based in Beirut, Lebanon. We help people buy, sell, rent and let apartments, villas, land and commercial property across Beirut and Mount Lebanon. This policy covers our website, elevateestateslb.com, and the enquiries that reach us through it, including through WhatsApp, phone, Facebook and Instagram.

When this policy says “we”, “us” or “our”, it means Elevate Estates. We are responsible for the personal information described here.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Buyers and tenants: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, and what you are looking for, such as area, budget, property type and move-in date.
  • Property owners and landlords: your contact details, the property’s address or area, asking price or rent, size, layout, features, and the photos you upload. We may also ask for proof that you own the property or are authorised to market it.
  • Agent applicants: the contact and background details you enter on the application form.
  • Messages: anything you send us by WhatsApp, phone, Facebook Messenger or Instagram.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, IP address, approximate location, the pages you view and the buttons you click (for example “Contact on WhatsApp”).
  • Information collected by the Meta Pixel, described in section 6.

We do not ask for payment card details on this website. We do not ask for sensitive information such as religion, health or political views, and we ask you not to send it.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries, share matching properties and arrange viewings.
  • To review, publish and market property listings, on this website and on our social media pages.
  • To put buyers and tenants in touch with owners and landlords, and to support negotiations, offers and contracts.
  • To assess applications from agents who want to join us.
  • To measure and improve our advertising on Facebook and Instagram, including showing our listings to people who visited the site.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent fraud and fake listings, and meet our legal obligations.

We only send you property updates or marketing if you asked for them or are already our client, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

Who we share it with

  • The other side of a deal. If you enquire about a property, we may share your name and contact details with its owner or landlord to arrange a viewing or pass on an offer. We share owners’ details with buyers or tenants only when it is needed to move a deal forward.
  • Professionals involved in a transaction. For example lawyers, notaries, banks or surveyors, when you ask us to proceed.
  • Service providers who run parts of the site for us: Vercel (website hosting), Supabase (storing form submissions and photos), Google Fonts (typefaces), and Meta (advertising measurement). They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

Property details and photos you submit for a listing will be shown publicly once we publish it. We do not publish the owner’s name or phone number on the listing.

Some of these providers store data outside Lebanon. When that happens, we rely on their security commitments to protect it.

Meta Pixel & advertising

This website uses the Meta Pixel, a piece of code from Meta Platforms (the company behind Facebook and Instagram). It records actions such as viewing a property, searching listings, submitting a listing or tapping to contact us on WhatsApp or by phone. Meta receives this together with information about your browser and may link it to your Facebook or Instagram account.

We use it to:

  • Understand which of our ads lead people to view or enquire about properties.
  • Show our listings to people who have visited the site, and to people with similar interests.

We do not send Meta your name, phone number or email from our forms. Meta’s own use of this data is covered by the Meta Privacy Policy.

Opting out. You can control how Meta uses website activity for ads in your Facebook ad preferences and review it under Your activity off Meta technologies. You can also block third-party cookies in your browser or use an ad blocker.

Cookies & local storage

The Meta Pixel sets cookies (such as _fbp) to recognise your browser across visits. The site also keeps a copy of the current listings in your browser’s local storage so pages load faster, and a random visit ID in session storage that is erased when you close the tab. We use that visit ID to count clicks on buttons such as “Contact on WhatsApp”. It is not linked to your name or number.

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time from your browser settings. The site will still work if you do.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: up to 24 months after our last contact, so we can follow up on properties that match what you asked for.
  • Listings: while the property is on the market, and up to 24 months after it is sold, rented or withdrawn. Closed deals may stay on our Recently Sold page without any owner details.
  • Agent applications: up to 12 months after a decision.
  • Transaction records: for as long as Lebanese law requires us to keep them.

After that, we delete the information or remove anything that identifies you.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Form submissions are stored with access limited to Elevate Estates staff who need it. No system is completely secure, so please do not send us passwords, ID scans or bank details through the website. If we need documents to complete a deal, we will tell you how to share them safely.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy.
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date, including your listing details.
  • Delete your information, or take your property listing down.
  • Stop sending you property updates or marketing.
  • Stop using your information for a particular purpose.

We will reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first, for example by replying from the phone number you used with us. We may keep some information where the law requires it.

Deleting your data

To have your personal information deleted, including anything collected through our Facebook or Instagram pages or ads:

  • Send a WhatsApp message to +961 71 991 088 saying “Data deletion request”, with your name and the phone number or email you used with us.
  • Or send the same request by direct message to our Facebook page or Instagram.

We will confirm once your data is deleted, within 30 days. If you have a listing with us, deleting your data also takes the listing down.

To remove the activity Meta holds about your visits to this site, use Your activity off Meta technologies in your Facebook settings.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we will post a notice on the site.
